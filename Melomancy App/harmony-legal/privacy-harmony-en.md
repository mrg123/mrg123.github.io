# Melomancy Privacy Policy (HarmonyOS Edition)

**Effective Date: September 12, 2026 | Platform: HarmonyOS (Huawei AppGallery)**

## Introduction

Melomancy (the "App") is operated by the Melomancy Development Team ("we", "us"). We treat your personal information with great care. This policy explains what information we collect in the HarmonyOS edition, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you may exercise.

By using the App you agree to the information practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App and uninstall it.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the App's features. We do not collect information unrelated to the service, we do not serve advertising, and we do not track you across other apps.

### 1. Account Information

The App signs you in with your **Huawei ID**. With your authorization, we receive the account identifier returned by Huawei Account Kit (used to create and identify your account) together with the nickname and avatar you authorize. You may change your nickname and avatar in the App at any time.

### 2. Avatar Image

You may set an avatar through the system gallery picker. The selected image is used only to display your avatar; the selection is handled by a system component (no gallery permission required), and the App neither reads your gallery in bulk nor accesses images you did not select.

### 3. Subscription Information

We record your account's subscription status and expiration date in order to grant and maintain subscription benefits. **In-app purchase is not yet available in the HarmonyOS edition.** Once it becomes available, payment will be processed by Huawei AppGallery; we will only receive the subscription product identifier and its validity period, and will never have access to your payment account, bank card, or other payment details.

### 4. Feature Usage Data

To provide and improve the service, we record:

- **Fortune draw history**: your daily draws (linked to your account, shown on the history page);
- **Favorites**: the albums, tracks and covers you favorite;
- **Cover tasks**: the parameters of cover tasks you start (source track, style) and their results;
- **Play counts**: aggregate per-track play counters. These are accumulated per track only, are not linked to your account, and cannot be used to identify you.

### 5. Device Permissions

All permissions requested by the App are used to deliver the corresponding feature and do not involve collecting personal information:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Network access (INTERNET) | Access online services such as the music library, fortune draws and cover generation |
| Network status (GET_NETWORK_INFO) | Detect network availability so the App can notify you when offline and refresh automatically after reconnection |
| Vibration (VIBRATE) | Haptic feedback for fortune draws, playback and favorites |
| Background running (KEEP_BACKGROUND_RUNNING) | Continue playing music (including lock-screen controls) when the App is in the background |

These are normal-level permissions that take effect upon installation. They do not trigger permission dialogs and do not upload any personal information.

### 6. Logs and Technical Data

Our servers keep standard access logs (such as timestamps and request paths) for troubleshooting and security auditing.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

1. Create and maintain your account and sign-in state;
2. Display your history, favorites and covers;
3. Record and maintain subscription benefits;
4. Generate fortune texts and compute track popularity (for recommendation ranking);
5. Keep the service secure and stable.

We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

## Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Huawei**: Huawei ID sign-in and AppGallery distribution;
- **Large language model services**: used to generate fortune texts. We submit only the fortune level, album name and target language; no account identifier or personal identity information is included;
- **Cloud computing services**: used to host our servers.

## Storage and Protection

- Your information is stored on our servers; communication between the App and the servers is encrypted in transit;
- Sign-in credentials are protected by signature mechanisms, and access tokens have a limited lifetime;
- Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel under reasonable access controls.

## Retention and Deletion

- **Account data**: you may delete your account at any time via "Me → Settings → Delete Account". Once deleted, your account information, fortune history, favorites and cover task records are deleted or anonymized and cannot be recovered;
- **Audio files**: generated cover audio may remain on our servers as part of the music library, but it contains no personal identity information and is no longer linked to your account;
- **Logs**: standard logs are retained for no longer than 90 days.

## Minors

The App is not designed for children under 14. If you are a minor, please use the App under the guidance of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without guardian consent, we will delete it promptly.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal information:

- **Nickname and avatar**: editable at any time via "Me → Edit Profile";
- **Favorites and fortune history**: deletable by you on the relevant pages;
- **Account and all linked data**: deletable in one step via "Settings → Delete Account", or by contacting us using the details below.

## Policy Updates

We may revise this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App. Continuing to use the App means you accept the revised policy.
